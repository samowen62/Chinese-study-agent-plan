# Progress Log

Append one line per generated unit. The 温故知新 (review) section of each new worksheet pulls
from the most recent line, one from ~3 lines back, and one from ~7–10 lines back
(see `syllabuses/01-hsk-syllabus.md` §Review protocol). "needs review" comes from Sam's
self-check strip / score-summary report — that content is REQUIRED in the next worksheet's
review section and re-tested in the next GT/VT.

Format:
`date | worksheet_id | type | level | unit (points / cluster / batch topic) | score | verdict | needs_review`

- type: P (practice) · GT (grammar test) · VT (vocabulary test)
- score/verdict: P worksheets leave as `—`; GT/VT record `X/Y (Z%)` + pass/fail once Sam
  reports (80% pass guideline); retakes replace the failed attempt's row.
- needs_review: missed points (`data-point`) or words (`data-word`) from tests; words from
  P worksheets' 词语收藏 notes.
