HSK 3 · 把字句（一）

语法点 L3-SP-1 把字句1 话题 家庭生活 · 搬家与整理房间 用时 约 13 分钟
学习目标：能用"把"字句说明某人把东西怎么处理了、处理成什么样了 —— Use 把-sentences to say what someone did to an object and where / how it ended up.

A 模式感知 Pattern warm-up · 点开卡片；虚线汉字悬停显示拼音

Click a card to flip. Hover a dashed word to see pinyin.

我把新的地图挂在墙上了。
S＋把＋O＋V＋在/到＋处所
把 = the action was applied to the object, and it ended up somewhere.
请把箱子放到阳台上。
Same pattern in a request. 把 must be followed by an action plus result/location — never a bare verb.
他打破了我的杯子。
This sentence is fine without 把 — but once you want to highlight "what happened to MY CUP", you repackage it: 他把我的杯子打破了。

用法例句 — 不同场合。Hover for pinyin.

1. 请把这份报告交给经理。 — Please hand this report to the manager. 职场

2. 他把行李放在出租车后面了。 — He put the luggage behind the taxi. 出行

3. 我把鱼做成汤了。 — I made the fish into soup. 饮食

B 语法操练 Grammar drills · 从易到难

从易到难，做完一题再点"对答案"。Do them in order; check each answer before moving on.

B1 哪个句子是对的？

A ✔ 把 is followed by object＋verb＋result (做完). — B fails because 把-sentences need a result/complement after the verb: a bare 做 is incomplete. C fails because 想 (to miss) is a feeling, not an action you "apply" to an object like 家. D fails on word order: 把 must come after the subject and before its object.

B2 选词填空：我把信（　）给王老师了。

C ✔ Pattern ② : S＋把＋O1＋V（＋给）＋O2 — I handed the letter TO teacher Wang. — 在/到 introduce a location, but 王老师 is a person receiving the object, so you need 给 (attached to the verb) instead. 交了了 would be ungrammatical; 交了 alone loses the recipient.

B3 排列顺序：把这些词排成一句话。Click the words in order.

我把房间打扫干净了。 Subject → 把 → object → verb＋result. The trap: 干净了 (clean) is the result, so it must come after the verb 打扫 — never "我打扫干净了把房间".

B4 找错误：下面这句话有一个错，选出来。 Choose the wrong part.

他 把 我的房间 没打扫 干净。

D ✔ In a 把-sentence, 不/没 must stand BEFORE 把: 他没把我的房间打扫干净。 Negating after 把 is one of the most common learner errors.

B5 用"把"改写：他关上了门。→

他把门关上了。 Model answer. Acceptable: 他把门给关上了 (colloquial 给). Wrong version to avoid: 他把关门了 — 把 must be followed directly by its object 门.

C 听力模拟 Listening simulation · read-aloud dialogue

Read the dialogue aloud twice — once for meaning, once at natural speed. (🔊 reads it aloud if your browser supports it.) Then answer C1–C3.

女:喂，你在哪儿呢？我们到家了。
男:我把车停在楼下了，马上上来。
女:哎，我把箱子放在门口了，太重了，我搬不动。
男:你先把东西放到客厅去吧。
女:那个书架呢？妈妈把它放在阳台上了。
男:好。哎呀，我把你妈妈的花瓶忘在车上了！
女:那你快去拿，小心别把它打碎了！

C1 男的把什么忘在车上了？

B ✔ 我把你妈妈的花瓶忘在车上了 — a perfect 把 pattern ① with 在＋处所. 箱子 was placed at the door by the woman; the bookshelf is on the balcony.

C2 判断对错：妈妈把书架放在客厅了。（√ / ×）

× ✔ 妈妈把它放在阳台上了， not the living room — the location after 在 is exactly what a 把-sentence locks in.

C3 女的最后一句"小心别把它打碎了"里的"它"是什么？

花瓶 (the vase). "它" refers to the thing being carried back from the car — the vase. Note the negative command with 把: 别＋把＋O＋V＋result.

D 考试题型 Exam-style · HSK3 选词填空

每个词只能用一次。Click a bank word, then click the blank. (HSK3 阅读 · 第二部分 format)

1. 我的手机 弟弟弄坏了。

2. 请把这封信交 王老师。

3. 老师 我们把课文读了两遍。

4. 快点儿，别 书忘在教室里了。

1-B 被 2-C 给 3-D 让 4-A 把（E 在、F 从 不用）
1. 被 passive: the phone suffered the action. 2. 交＋给＋person = hand TO. 3. 让＋O＋V = have someone do something. 4. 别＋把＋书＋忘＋在＋处所 — negative command with 把.
弄坏 nòng huài = break/damage (HSK4 word, glossed as promised for anything above level).

E 自由产出 Free production

E1 你昨天到家以后，先把什么东西放在哪儿了？用一个"把"字句回答。

Model: 我先把书包放在椅子上了。 Variants: 我到家后把外套挂在了门上。 Check yourself: does the verb have a result or location after it? If you wrote 我把钥匙放 — incomplete; it needs 放在了哪儿 or 放好了.

E2 Translate into Chinese: "I left your book at school."

Model: 我把你的书忘在学校了。 Over-literal to avoid: 我忘了你的书在学校 — Chinese needs 把 to move 你的书 before the verb; 忘 in this pattern takes 在＋place as the result.

F 温故知新 Review · from earlier worksheets

F1 选正确答案：今天跟昨天（　）。from hsk3-ws-013 · 比较句2

A ✔ A跟B一样＋形容词. C adds an unnecessary 不, changing the meaning to "as cold as NOT…". D would need 冷得跟昨天一样.

F2 用"一边……一边……"把两个句子合成一句话：我打扫房间。我听音乐。from hsk3-ws-012 · 一边……一边……

Model: 我一边打扫房间一边听音乐。 Both actions happen at the same time, same subject.

F3 用"越来越"完成这句话：我把房间打扫干净以后，房间（　）。from hsk3-ws-011 · 越来越

Model: 房间越来越干净了。 — notice how today's 把-sentence (我把房间打扫干净了) and the old pattern 越来越 connect naturally.
