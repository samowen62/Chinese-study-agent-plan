HSK 4 · 词汇测试 01 · 个人情况

话题 日常生活 · 个人情况 词量 27 个（20 个四级 + 7 个复习词） 用时 约 14 分钟
学习目标：掌握 27 个描述"人"的词 —— 年龄、性格、外貌和感受。每个词先学，再认、再读、再用。

A 词语讲解 Word definitions · 每个词都要过一遍（约 2–3 分钟）

年龄名 #1536 age, how old someone is.
① 报名表要写真实年龄。 — Put your real age on the form. 职场
② 坐飞机，儿童按年龄买票。 — Kids buy plane tickets by age. 出行

性别名 #1829 gender (male/female).
① 体检表上要填性别。 — Fill in gender on the medical form. 职场
② 这门课不限性别，谁都能报。 — The class has no gender limit. 学习

性格名 #1831 personality, character.
① 经理的性格很开朗，开会不怕说话。 — The manager's personality is outgoing. 职场
② 她的性格内向，不爱在食堂聊天。 — She's introverted and doesn't chat in the cafeteria. 饮食

脾气名 #1552 temper. 搭配：发脾气.
① 客户一催，他就爱发脾气。 — He loses his temper when clients chase him. 职场
② 堵车的时候别发脾气。 — Don't lose your temper in a traffic jam. 出行

帅形 #1695 handsome (for men).
① 他穿西装去面试，特别帅。 — He looked handsome in a suit at the interview. 职场
② 列车员那身制服挺帅的。 — That conductor's uniform looks pretty sharp. 出行

美形 #1500 beautiful.
① 窗外的风景真美。 — The view out the window is beautiful. 出行
② 这盘菜颜色很美，先别动筷子。 — This dish looks beautiful — don't start yet. 饮食

笨形 #1026 slow to understand, "dumb".
① 软件不难，别说自己笨。 — The software isn't hard; don't call yourself slow. 职场
② 这道语法不难，你一点儿也不笨。 — This grammar isn't hard; you're not slow at all. 学习

诚实形 #1081 honest.
① 面试时要诚实，别夸大经历。 — Be honest in the interview; don't inflate your record. 职场
② 他很诚实，作业不会做就直接说。 — He's honest — if he can't do the homework, he says so. 学习

粗心形 #1106 careless. 反义词：细心.
① 他太粗心，把附件发错了。 — He was so careless he sent the wrong attachment. 职场
② 考试时粗心，把题号填错了。 — Careless in the exam, he marked the wrong item numbers. 学习

细心形 #1792 careful, attentive to detail.
① 会计很细心，账目一个错都没有。 — The accountant is careful; the books have no errors. 职场
② 她细心地把生词都记在本子上。 — She carefully copied every new word into her notebook. 学习

活泼形 #1313 lively, outgoing.
① 新同事很活泼，茶水间总有笑声。 — The new colleague is lively; the pantry is always laughing. 职场
② 导游很活泼，一路讲故事。 — The guide is lively and tells stories the whole way. 出行

幽默形 #1882 humorous, funny (pleasant).
① 老板说话很幽默，开会不那么紧张。 — The boss is humorous; meetings feel lighter. 职场
② 这本课文的对话很幽默。 — This textbook dialogue is humorous. 学习

害羞形 #1284 shy.
① 第一次见客户，他害羞得不敢说话。 — Meeting a client the first time, he was too shy to talk. 职场
② 她害羞，点菜总让别人先说。 — She's shy and always lets others order first. 饮食

骄傲形/名 #1368 proud (often negative: too pleased with yourself).
① 项目成功了也不能骄傲。 — Even after a successful project, don't get smug. 职场
② 考了满分，他有点儿骄傲。 — He scored full marks and got a bit proud. 学习

耐心形/名 #1521 patient; 有耐心 = have patience.
① 教新同事用系统要有耐心。 — Teaching a new colleague the system takes patience. 职场
② 背生词要有耐心，一天记不完。 — Memorizing words takes patience; you can't finish in a day. 学习

自信形/名 #1983 confident. 注意：自信≠骄傲.
① 答辩的时候要自信。 — Be confident in the presentation. 职场
② 口语考试时，自信比语法更重要。 — In the oral test, confidence matters more than grammar. 学习

懒形 #1452 lazy. 搭配：懒得+V.
① 周末他懒得开会，把材料提前发了。 — He couldn't be bothered to meet; he sent the materials ahead. 职场
② 我懒得做饭，就点了快餐。 — I couldn't be bothered to cook, so I ordered fast food. 饮食

眼镜名 #1852 glasses. 搭配：戴眼镜.
① 看合同要戴眼镜。 — Wear glasses to read the contract. 职场
② 他开车忘了戴眼镜。 — He forgot his glasses when driving. 出行

皮肤名 #1551 skin.
① 空调房里皮肤容易干。 — Skin dries out in air-conditioned offices. 职场
② 辣菜吃多了，皮肤会红。 — Too much spicy food can make your skin flush. 饮食

印象名 #1873 impression. 搭配：给…留下好印象 / 对…的印象.
① 第一次见客户，要留下好印象。 — First meeting a client, leave a good impression. 职场
② 我对这个城市的印象是又干净又方便。 — My impression of the city is clean and convenient. 出行

高兴形 [L1] happy (review).
① 合同签成了，全组都很高兴。 — The contract was signed; the whole team was happy. 职场
② 火车准点到，旅客都高兴。 — The train arrived on time; passengers were happy. 出行

漂亮形 [L1] pretty (review).

聪明形 [L3] smart (review).
① 这个办法很聪明，能省一半时间。 — This method is smart; it saves half the time. 职场

忙形 [L1] busy (review).
① 月底财务很忙，别随便请假。 — Finance is busy at month-end; don't take random leave. 职场
② 春节前车站特别忙。 — Stations are especially busy before Spring Festival. 出行

岁量 [L1] years of age (review). 搭配：数字＋岁.
① 新同事才二十二岁，已经能独立做项目。 — The new colleague is only 22 and already runs projects. 职场
② 孩子不满六岁可以免票。 — Kids under six ride free. 出行

C 认词 Meaning recognition

C1 "性格"是什么意思？

B ✔ 性格 = the way a person behaves and feels — personality. 年龄 age, 性别 gender, 皮肤 skin (same batch distractors).

C2 "耐心"是什么意思？

A ✔ 耐心 = able to wait calmly. 懒 lazy, 骄傲 proud, 害羞 shy — all batch adjectives describing people, but only 耐心 is "patient".

C3 "印象"是什么意思？

C ✔ 印象 = the feeling/memory a person or thing leaves you. 我对他印象很好.

C4 哪个词的意思是 "stupid / slow to understand"（笨）？

A ✔ 笨 bèn. 诚实 honest, 活泼 lively, 幽默 humorous — opposite-direction character words.

C5 你想夸一个男同学长得好看，用哪个词？

B ✔ 帅 is for men; 美/漂亮 usually for women or scenery. 高兴 is a feeling.

C6 哪个词的意思是 "confident"（有信心）？

D ✔ 自信 = believe in yourself (positive). 骄傲 is proud in the negative "too pleased" sense; 害羞 shy; 粗心 careless.

D 读音 Pinyin ↔ character

D1 "性格"的正确拼音是？

C ✔ 性 xìng (4th) + 格 gé (2nd) — both full tones. ✗xīngé misreads 性 as 1st tone.

D2 "脾气"的正确拼音是？

A ✔ 气 is a light (neutral) tone here: píqi. Writing píqì isn't "wrong" in some dictionaries, but HSK convention reads it neutral.

D3 "骄傲"的正确拼音是？

B ✔ Both syllables are 1st + 4th: jiāo'ào. Watch the apostrophe — without it "jiāoào" is unreadable.

D4 "眼镜"的正确拼音是？

C ✔ 眼 yǎn (3rd) + 镜 jìng (4th). B yǎnjīng misplaces the tone mark syllable.

E 用法 Usage in context · 每个词只能用一次

1. 她的很活泼，跟谁都聊得来。

2. 别发，先听我解释。

3. 教爷爷奶奶用手机要有。

4. 他很，从来不说谎。

5. 姐姐工作很，一个错误都没出过。

1-A 性格 · 2-B 脾气 · 3-C 耐心 · 4-E 诚实 · 5-F 细心（D 年龄 不用）
Cues: ① 性格+活泼 is the standard collocation ② 发脾气 fixed phrase ③ 有耐心 ④ 诚实＋不说谎 ⑤ 细心＋不出错. 年龄 needs a number context (他的年龄是…).

E6 他说话总能把大家逗笑，大家都很喜欢他。他是个很（　）的人。

B ✔ Making people laugh = 幽默. A shy person wouldn't be the class comedian.

F 造句 Production · self-graded

F1 用"印象"写一句话。

Model: 第一次见面，他给我留下了很好的印象。 Variants: 我对这个城市的印象是又大又热闹。 Avoid ✗我印象他 — needs 对…的印象 or 给我留下印象.

F2 用今天学过的两个词描述一个你认识的人（性格、脾气、幽默、耐心…任选）。

Model: 我爸爸脾气很好，也很有耐心，从来不发火。 Check your two words are used with their right collocation (发脾气 / 有耐心 / 性格+Adj).

F3 情景：朋友穿上新西装，你想夸他。用"帅"说一句话。

Model: 哇，你穿这件西装太帅了！ Variants: 你今天真帅。 Common mistake: ✗你很帅死了 — 帅死了 works, 很…死了 doesn't (pick one intensifier).
