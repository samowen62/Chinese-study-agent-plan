B 摸底题 · Placement items auto-scored

1 场景：你昨天写完了作业，想强调"作业已经被我处理完了"。你说：我已经把作业（　）完了。

A ✔ 把字句：S＋把＋O＋V＋结果。"把作业做完" = the action was applied to the object and finished it. 被做 (passive marker can't follow the object inside 把), 再做 (do again — wrong meaning), 在做 (in the middle of doing, clashes with 完了).

2 场景：你想说"汉语的难度一年年在增加"：汉语（　）难了。

C ✔ 越来越＋Adj = degree increases over time. 一边…一边… is two simultaneous actions; 先…再… is sequencing; 还是 offers a choice or "had better".

3 场景：今天 38 度，你想夸张地说热到极点：今天热（　）！

B ✔ Adj＋死了 = extremely (spoken, vivid): 热死了／累死了／忙死了. The complement attaches directly — no 得, and 死 must come before 了. This is L4 cluster CL01, coming up in your first practice unit.

4 场景：班里 20 个学生，有 6 个是留学生。你说：我们班有二十个学生，（　）六个是留学生。

A ✔ 其中 = "of which / among them", picks a subset out of a just-mentioned group. 所有 = all (needs 的＋noun), 任何 = any, 各种 = various kinds. (CL06 merges into the CL22 unit later.)

5 场景：这道题非常难，连老师都答不上来。你想强调"老师也…"，应选：

C ✔ 连＋X＋都＋V = "even X" — escalates to an extreme example (even the teacher can't). 既…又… lists two qualities; 一…就… links two quick events; 把 is the disposal construction.

6 "这不是你写的信吗？" 这句话是什么问句？

B ✔ 不是……吗？ is a rhetorical question: the speaker is sure ("Of course it's the letter you wrote!"), no real answer expected. 是非问句 genuinely asks yes/no; 特指 asks with 谁/什么; 正反问 uses V不V.
